# Commute and Transport Policy

Company: Acme Technologies (fictional sample company)
Version: 2026.1 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Facilities & Admin

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy covers employee commute on rostered shifts: shared cab pickups and drops, the shuttle loop, and ad-hoc cab bookings coordinated through the transport desk. It applies to full-time Acme employees at the Whitefield campus. Guest travel and guest-ride reimbursement are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Eligibility

Employees in grades L1 to L5 who live within 15 km of the Whitefield campus are eligible for rostered transport. Employees above L5, interns, and contractors are not eligible. Eligibility is checked at enrolment against the address on record; a change of address must be reported to the transport desk within 10 working days.

## 3. Shift Timing and Booking Windows

Rostered pickups are scheduled so that employees reach the office 15 minutes before shift start. Drops for shifts ending between 6:00 PM and 8:00 PM are served by the regular cab roster. Booking changes are accepted until 9:00 PM the previous day in the MoveInSync app; later changes count as ad-hoc requests.

## 4. Night Drops and Security Escorts

For women employees, a security escort is mandatory on any drop that begins after 8:00 PM. The escort boards first and exits last, and the cab cannot be shared with other employees on such drops. Male employees on shifts ending after 8:00 PM are dropped by the regular roster; an escort is not provided for them.

## 5. Weekend and Holiday Service

Saturday pickups are available only with prior manager approval recorded in the app. Sunday service is not provided. On public holidays notified by HR, only the reduced holiday shuttle timetable operates; rostered cabs do not run.

## 6. Ad-hoc Trips and Airport Travel

Ad-hoc trips, meaning any trip not on the roster, must be requested at least 3 hours in advance. Airport trips are approved by the transport desk; no other approval is valid for airport travel. Requests without approval are declined automatically by the app.

## 7. Monthly Cost Cap

Ad-hoc trip costs are reimbursable up to ₹900 per employee per calendar month. Amounts above the cap are billed to the employee. Rostered commute costs do not count against the cap.

## 8. No-shows and Penalties

A no-show is recorded when the employee is not at the pickup point within 5 minutes of cab arrival. After 2 no-shows in a calendar month, the employee's booking access is suspended for 7 days. The suspension lifts automatically after 7 days; repeated suspensions within a quarter are reviewed by the transport desk.
