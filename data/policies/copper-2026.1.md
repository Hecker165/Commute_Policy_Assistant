# Commute and Transport Policy

Company: Copper Logistics (fictional sample company)
Version: 2026.1 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: Administration Team

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy covers shared cab transport for Copper Logistics shift staff, the plant shuttle between the metro station and the warehouse, and approved ad-hoc trips booked through the transport desk. Guest travel and guest-ride reimbursement are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Eligibility

All permanent employees working at the Peenya warehouse who live within 12 km of the designated pickup corridors are eligible. Trainees are eligible only for the plant shuttle, not for door pickup. Eligibility is reviewed every January against the address record.

## 3. Shift Timing and Booking Windows

Door pickups operate for shifts starting between 05:00 and 22:00. Employees must be at the pickup point 10 minutes before the scheduled time. Roster changes close at 8:00 PM on the previous day; requests after that are handled as ad-hoc trips by the transport desk.

## 4. Night Drops and Security Escorts

For women employees on shifts ending after 9:00 PM, a security escort is mandatory on the drop, and the cab must drop her first before other employees. Drops that begin before 9:00 PM run on the regular shared roster without an escort.

## 5. Weekend and Holiday Service

Saturday pickups operate normally for employees rostered on weekend shifts; no separate approval is required beyond the roster itself. Sunday pickups run only where the shift roster explicitly lists Sunday duty. On notified holidays the plant shuttle still runs, but door pickups are suspended.

## 6. Ad-hoc Trips and Airport Travel

Ad-hoc trips are booked by the transport desk on request, subject to vehicle availability. Airport trips require written approval from the head of the employee's department before the booking is confirmed. The transport desk cannot approve airport trips on its own.

## 7. Monthly Cost Cap

Ad-hoc trips are reimbursed up to ₹1,200 per employee per calendar month. Rostered pickups and drops are free of charge and do not count toward the cap. Trips booked without approval are not reimbursed at all.

## 8. No-shows and Penalties

A no-show is recorded when the employee is unreachable at the pickup point for 10 minutes after the scheduled time. From the third no-show in a calendar month onward, a fare penalty of ₹500 is charged for each no-show. There is no booking suspension under this policy; persistent absence is escalated to the department head.
