# Commute and Transport Policy

Company: Bluebird Transport (fictional sample company)
Version: 2026.1 · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Owner: HR Operations

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy covers the employee shuttle network, fixed-route last-mile cabs, and rostered shift transport operated for Bluebird staff across the Hitech City corridor. Guest travel and guest-ride reimbursement are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Eligibility

All permanent employees in bands A1 to A3 who live within 20 km of the Hitech City office are eligible for the shuttle network and assigned last-mile connections. Consultants and contractors are not eligible. Enrolment is confirmed by HR once the verified address is on file.

## 3. Shift Timing and Booking Windows

Shuttle runs align to standard shifts starting at 07:30, 09:30, 14:00 and 19:30. Employees are picked up so as to arrive 20 minutes before shift start. Route or shift changes must be entered before 7:00 PM on the previous day; same-day changes are treated as ad-hoc requests.

## 4. Night Drops and Security Escorts

A security escort is mandatory for women employees on any drop that begins after 10:00 PM. On escorted drops the cab is exclusive: it cannot be shared with other employees, and the escort remains with the employee until she enters her residence gate. Employees whose shifts end before 10:00 PM travel by the regular roster without an escort.

## 5. Weekend and Holiday Service

Saturday pickups and drops are not provided; the shuttle network and rostered cabs operate Monday to Friday only. Employees rostered on Saturdays use personal conveyance and apply under the separate HR travel reimbursement process. Shuttle service is suspended on public holidays notified by HR.

## 6. Ad-hoc Trips and Airport Travel

Ad-hoc cab requests are limited to two per month and must be raised before 6:00 PM on the prior day. Airport trips are approved by the line manager, who confirms the flight details before the booking is made. Requests without line-manager approval are not booked.

## 7. Monthly Cost Cap

Reimbursable ad-hoc transport is capped at ₹750 per employee per calendar month. Claims above the cap require written HR exception approval and are not paid by default. Rostered shuttle costs are borne by the company and do not count toward the cap.

## 8. No-shows and Penalties

A no-show is recorded when the employee does not board within 5 minutes of the scheduled pickup time. After 3 no-shows in a rolling 30-day window, the case goes to the employee's manager for review, and recurring offenders may lose ad-hoc booking rights at the manager's discretion.
